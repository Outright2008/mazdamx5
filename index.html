History of the MX-5

First manufactured in 1989, the MX-5 Mk1 had an iconic design that made it an immediate hit with car enthusiasts around the world. It was first manufactured with a 1.6L engine, but later models were upgraded to a 1.8L engine for improved performance. The MX-5 is also known for its lightweight construction and rear wheel drive, making it an absolute joy to drive.

recent history

In recent years, the MX-5 has only grown in popularity and has amassed a devoted fanbase worldwide - commondly referred to as the "perfect starter sports car"
